## Change the account at any time

The entry of the integration now has **Reconfigurar** in its three-dot menu, to change the document number, the password or the profile whenever you want (#23).

- Until now they could only be entered again after the portal had refused a login.
- The history is kept, and the session helper tries the login again even if it had given up after a refusal.
- Use it when you change your password at the virtual office, without waiting for a failure.

The README has a short section, "Cambiar la cuenta o los ajustes", on where each setting lives.

No other changes. Update the integration and restart Home Assistant.
