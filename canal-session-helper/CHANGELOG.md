## New icons

- The icon is now the Canal's wave symbol alone, without the lettering, so it can be recognised at icon size.
- Icons and logos have a transparent background, and dark themes get a logo with white lettering. No more white box.
- The same images are used by the session helper app (#22).

## Documentation

- A new section in the README, [El captcha del portal: qué puede fallar](https://github.com/miguelangel-nubla/homeassistant_canal_isabel_II#el-captcha-del-portal-qué-puede-fallar), explains the invisible captcha that guards the login, that it can start asking for challenges or refusing at any time, and what helps: above all, not using the virtual office or the Canal's app while the integration is logging in.

No functional changes. Update the integration and restart Home Assistant; the browser may keep the old icons for a while.
